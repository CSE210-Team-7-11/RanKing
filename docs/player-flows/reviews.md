# Player reviews

[Player flows](README.md)

Store vetting establishes that a game is playable and accurately described. Reviews let someone considering it hear from players who tried it. Eligibility therefore depends on actual play, while editing lets feedback change as a player gains experience or the game receives updates.

## Becoming eligible

A signed-in player can review after a meaningful interaction that the game reports to the app. Practice qualifies. A player who reaches that point and leaves early can also review, since confusing controls or an unclear objective may be exactly what they want to report. Requiring a completed or ranked round would exclude some of that feedback.

[Steam reviews](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/reviews) provides a reference for tying recommendations to play experience.

The game identifies the meaningful interaction because it knows its mechanics. The app records the build the player tried so that the review can describe a particular version.

## Leaving a recommendation

> **S31** As a player who has tried a game, I want to say whether I recommend it and explain my experience if I choose.

After eligible play or an eligible early exit, **Review game** appears as a secondary action. The player can keep playing and review later from the Store listing. This makes feedback available without inserting a mandatory step into replay.

The form asks **Would you recommend this game?** with **Yes** and **No**. Written feedback is optional. A player can contribute a recommendation without composing a review, while someone with a specific experience to explain can add text.

Each account contributes one recommendation per game across versions. The listing shows the recommendation proportion and number of votes, including when there are none. Written reviews show the played build, for example **Played version 1.2**, so a reader can assess whether an older complaint may still apply.

## Revising feedback

> **S48** As a player who has previously reviewed a game and whose experience has changed, I want to revise my recommendation or review.

**Edit your review** opens the existing recommendation and text. Saving changes replaces that account's previous vote rather than adding another. This lets a player respond to a fix without giving repeated reviewers more weight in the summary.

The version label identifies a build the reviewer actually played. Editing a review must preserve that relationship.
